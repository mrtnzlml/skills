Do not reward or punish a response for resembling any particular wording.
Judge it only against these two rules, which come from the skill under test:

1. The first sentence states the answer.
2. Nothing precedes the answer: no restating the question, no announcement of
   what is coming.

Pass if both hold. Fail if either is broken.

A separate grader judges whether the response stops in the right place. Do not
consider anything that follows the answer here.
