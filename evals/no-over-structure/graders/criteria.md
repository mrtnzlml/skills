A passing response answers the question directly in plain prose and does not
reach for a table, a bullet list, or headings to present three items it could
say in sentences.

Judge only the shape, not which job it picks. Any well-argued answer is fine,
including running the jobs in parallel. A necessary exception or condition is
not padding — the response may be longer than the bare answer when the extra
words change what is true.

Fail it only if a flowing explanation has been fragmented into scaffolding.
