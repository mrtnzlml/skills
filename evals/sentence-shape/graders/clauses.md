name: clauses not stacked
weight: 1
---

The reader has intermediate English. Do not judge sentence length or passive
voice — separate graders measure those.

Judge one thing only: no sentence stacks three or more clauses. A consequence
chained on with ", so" is a clause. A second "and" joining a new idea is a
clause.

Most sentences must hold. Fail the response only if any sentence stacks three
or more.
