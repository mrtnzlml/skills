The reader has intermediate English and is not a native speaker.

A passing answer says what happened in literal language. Every phrase means
what its words mean. Plain verbs like "found", "ran", "chose", "delayed" are
correct; so are ordinary combinations like "found out" or "set up".

Fail it if the answer uses a figure of speech a reader would have to look up —
an idiom, a metaphor, a sports or war comparison, or business jargon standing
in for a plain verb.
