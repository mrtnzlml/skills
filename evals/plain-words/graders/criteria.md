name: readable by a non-native speaker
weight: 2
---

The reader has intermediate English. They know the technical terms
(`--legacy-export`, `--export-profile`, "flag", "3.0") but not formal or
literary English.

A passing note uses the shortest common word for every non-technical idea:
"use" not "utilize", "before" not "prior to", "to" not "in order to",
"removed" not "deprecated and subsequently eliminated".

Fail it if a reader would have to look up a non-technical word, or if a
sentence needs a second pass to parse.
